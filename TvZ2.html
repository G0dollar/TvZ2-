📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Core Loop

🏠Start from the Main Menu. Choose Standard Battle or a Class Raid.
🎯Move your turret left/right and shoot zombies before they reach the bottom.
🔩Killing zombies drops Scrap (Iron, Steel, Titanium). Spend it on permanent upgrades.

🌊 Waves & Bosses

🌊Every 5 waves you face a Miniboss. Every 10 waves a Boss appears.
🏆Defeating a boss gives you a checkpoint choice — save & exit with 100%, or risk continuing for 1.5× resources.
💀If you die, you only keep 25% of your run's resources. Wave resets to the start of your current 5-wave block.

⚔️ Classes & Raids

🔥Raids earn Class Shards and Cores unique to that element.
🔓Spend 3 Broken Cores or 1 Full Core to unlock a class in the Class Manager.
⚡With a class active, earn Shards to buy powerful class upgrades that add special abilities to every shot.

🛡️ Emergency Exit

🏠Tap the 🏠 Home button mid-battle to start a 3-second escape. Survive and keep 50% of your run resources.
⚙️Upgrades bought in the Inventory are permanent and carry across all runs.
